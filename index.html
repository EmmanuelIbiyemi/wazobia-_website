Speak your language. Be understood..

Call anyone in Nigeria and talk in Yoruba or Igbo. The other person hears you in their own language, with no interpreter and no typing.

How it works

  1. Sign up and pick your language. Use your phone number, choose Yoruba or Igbo.
  2. Call or accept a call. Tap call on a contact, or tap green when someone rings you.
  3. Talk normally. You speak your language and the other person hears theirs.
Got an invite by SMS? Install the app and sign up with the same phone number the invite was sent to, so your friend can find you. Still in process sms not availiable

Installing on Android

Open the downloaded file and allow "install from this source" if your phone asks. This is an early version, so some features are still being added: more languages (Hausa, Pidgin) are coming next.
